# Chocolate-Sales-Dashboard
🍫 Awesome Chocolates Sales & Profit Dashboard

An interactive and visually engaging Power BI dashboard designed to analyze chocolate sales performance across multiple countries, products, and shipment trends. The dashboard provides business insights into sales, profit margins, shipment efficiency, and product-level profitability to support data-driven decision-making.

1. Project Title / Headline
🍫 Awesome Chocolates – Sales, Cost & Profit Analysis Dashboard

A dynamic Power BI dashboard built to monitor chocolate product performance across global markets with a focus on sales trends, shipment analysis, profitability, and operational efficiency.

2. Short Description / Purpose

The Awesome Chocolates Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report created to help businesses analyze sales, cost, shipment, and profit performance across different countries and chocolate product categories.

The dashboard enables users to track KPIs, compare product profitability, monitor shipment behavior, and identify high-performing products and regions through visually rich and intuitive analytics.

This dashboard is suitable for business analysts, sales managers, retail companies, and decision-makers who want quick insights into product performance and operational trends.

3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

📊 Power BI Desktop – Main platform used to build interactive reports and visualizations.
📂 Power Query – Used for data cleaning, transformation, and shaping.
🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for calculated measures, KPI indicators, conditional formatting, and dynamic calculations.
🔗 Data Modeling – Relationships created between sales, shipment, product, and calendar tables for efficient filtering and aggregation.
📈 Custom Visualizations – KPI cards, gauges, trend charts, and interactive tables used for storytelling and analysis.
📁 File Format – .pbix for development and .png for dashboard preview images.

4. Data Source
5. Source: Dataset provided by Chandoo.org Power BI Sample Dataset Repository

Description:
The dataset contains chocolate sales transactions including product details, shipment information, sales revenue, costs, profits, and regional performance data. It is commonly used for Power BI learning, dashboard development, and data visualization projects.
5. Features / Highlights
• Business Problem

The management team of Awesome Chocolates needs a centralized way to monitor business performance across multiple countries, products, and shipment operations.

Analyzing sales, profit, shipment volumes, and operational costs through spreadsheets makes it difficult to quickly identify trends and performance issues.

Key business questions include:

Which products generate the highest profit?
Which products have low profit margins despite strong sales?
How are shipment volumes distributed?
Are costs increasing over time?
Which countries contribute the most to business performance?
How is the business performing month-over-month?

The organization requires a visual analytics solution that enables quick monitoring and faster decision-making.

• Goal of the Dashboard

The primary goal of this dashboard is to provide a comprehensive view of sales, cost, shipment, and profitability performance for Awesome Chocolates.

The dashboard helps stakeholders:

Monitor key business KPIs in one place
Track month-over-month performance trends
Analyze product-level profitability
Identify operational inefficiencies
Compare performance across countries
Support data-driven business decisions through interactive visualizations

Total Sales
Total Boxes Sold
Total Shipments
Total Cost
Total Profit

Each KPI also displays Month-over-Month (MoM) performance, enabling users to quickly assess recent business trends.

The left-side country selector allows users to analyze performance across different regions:

Australia
Canada
India
New Zealand

🚚 Shipment Analysis

The shipment distribution chart provides insight into shipment volumes and delivery activity.

Users can:

Analyze shipment frequency
Monitor distribution patterns
Identify concentration of shipment volumes
Understand operational workload trends

The shipment gauge provides a quick performance indicator for shipment-related metrics.

🔄 Dynamic Metric Analysis

A Field Parameter combined with Power BI's Button Slicer enables users to dynamically switch between key business metrics:

Sales
Boxes
Shipments
Cost
Profit

Based on the selected metric, the trend chart updates automatically, allowing users to analyze different aspects of business performance within the same visual.

This approach improves user experience by reducing report clutter while providing flexible, self-service analysis.

To provide deeper insights, a custom report tooltip has been implemented. When users hover over a data point, the tooltip displays a geographical breakdown of the selected metric, helping users understand how each country contributes to overall performance without navigating away from the main report.

📖 Toggle Between  Product and Salesperson Performance Views

Using bookmark-based navigation icons, users can switch between two analytical views:

Product-wise Profit
Profit Percentage
LBS Contribution

Salesperson View

Salesperson-wise Sales
Profit Performance
Contribution Analysis
Comparative Ranking

This functionality enables users to evaluate performance from both operational and personnel perspectives within the same dashboard.
Conditional Performance Indicators

The dashboard uses visual indicators and conditional formatting to quickly highlight:

High-performing products/salespeople
Low-profit areas requiring attention
Strong profit margins
Underperforming segments

This helps decision-makers focus on exceptions and opportunities immediately.

• Business Impact & Insights

📈 Performance Monitoring

The dashboard provides a centralized view of key business KPIs, enabling stakeholders to monitor Sales, Profit, Cost, Shipments, and Boxes Sold in real time. Month-over-Month comparisons help identify performance improvements or declines quickly.

🌍 Geographic Performance Analysis

Country-level filtering and tooltip-based geographic insights allow users to compare business performance across markets such as India, USA, UK, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand. This helps identify high-performing regions and potential growth opportunities.

🍫 Product Portfolio Optimization

The product performance view highlights products that generate the highest sales and profit margins. Decision-makers can use these insights to focus on profitable products, optimize inventory planning, and review underperforming product lines.

👥 Sales Team Performance Evaluation

The salesperson analysis view enables managers to compare individual performance, identify top contributors, and recognize coaching opportunities for lower-performing team members.

🚚 Operational Efficiency Tracking

Shipment distribution analysis helps monitor fulfillment activity and shipment trends. This enables better understanding of operational workload and supports logistics planning.

🔍 Self-Service Business Analysis

Through the use of Field Parameters, Button Slicers, Bookmarks, and Interactive Tooltips, users can explore different business perspectives without requiring additional reports. This improves accessibility to insights and reduces dependency on manual analysis.

⚡ Faster Decision-Making

By consolidating multiple business metrics into a single interactive dashboard, stakeholders can move from data collection to decision-making more efficiently, improving responsiveness to changing business conditions.
